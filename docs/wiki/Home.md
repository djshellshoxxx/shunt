# SHUNT Wiki

SHUNT follows the tempo-master deck on a PRO DJ LINK network and republishes its tempo, beat and bar as
Ableton Link, MIDI clock and OSC, and logs what was played for cue sheets.

**Status:** early implementation. The repository holds the C++17 networking, clock-engine and tracklist
libraries, a CLI, a simulator and tests. The desktop app, plugin integrations and Raspberry Pi appliance
are planned, not released.
